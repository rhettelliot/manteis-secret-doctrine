# APPENDIX A: SOURCE TERM DECODING

> **Note:** The operator-facing text (Chapters 00–07) uses only operational language. This appendix decodes the original source terminology for those who wish to trace the concepts back to their origin. The original terms are provided here for reference and scholarly mapping only — they are not used in the main text.

## Terminology Map

| Source Term (Original) | Operational Term (Used in Text) | Plain English | Operational Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mulaprakriti | Root Substrate | Root matter / primal substance | Your foundational substrate — the assumptions, biology, and conditioning beneath your decisions |
| Parabrahm | The Absolute Context | The unknowable context | The substrate before distinction; not a thing you contact, but the ground that makes all contact possible |
| Akasa | Substrate Medium | Primordial substance / medium of thought | The informational field in which attention and intention propagate; the medium, not the message |
| Manvantara | Active Cycle | Cosmic day / active phase | A period of output, expansion, manifestation, and doing |
| Pralaya | Recovery Cycle | Cosmic night / rest phase | A period of recovery, integration, dissolution, and un-doing |
| Fohat | Execution Force | Cosmic electricity / active force | The executive energy that turns intention into action; willpower rendered as force |
| Kosmos | The Ordered System | The ordered universe | The structured reality you are operating inside; your system, organization, or life-as-system |
| Monad | Core Identity | The essential self | Your persistent core identity across roles, careers, and cycles; the witness and author of consequence |
| Manas | Cognitive Layer | Mind / the thinking principle | The reflective and discriminative capacity that separates reactive impulse from conscious choice |
| Buddhi | Intuitive Layer | Intuition / direct knowing | Direct pattern-recognition; knowing without exhaustive reasoning |
| Atma | Core Identity (ground) | Spirit / universal self | The witnessing ground; the part of awareness that is not identified with content |
| Root Race | Developmental Phase | Evolutionary epoch | A life-career phase with its own dominant principle, success metrics, and failure mode |
| Round | Cycle (recurring) | Cyclic progression through planes | A recurring life pattern that returns at progressively deeper levels until completed |
| Globe | Perspective | A plane or lens within a Cycle | A specific lens through which a recurring pattern can be viewed |
| Karma | Consequence | Causal consequence / action-law | The accumulated causal architecture that determines which patterns recur; uncompleted loops |
| Skandhas | Accumulated Dispositions | Aggregates / accumulated tendencies | The bundle of automatic responses, habits, and dispositions carried across cycles |
| Tanha | Attachment Driver | Thirst / craving / attachment | The clinging that keeps a cycle running by refusing to let a form complete or change |
| Adept | Conscious Operator | The master / the wise | The self-directed operator who has integrated the phases, cycles, and layers consciously |
| Dhyan-Chohan | Integrated Operator | Higher intelligence | A developmental possibility of the human system; what a human becomes when fully integrated |
| Stanzas of Dzyan | Seed Teachings | Source verses / axiomatic statements | Compressed statements of original principles; the axiomatic source from which doctrines unfold |
| Auric Field | Boundary Field | The human energy field | The personal boundary system; the selective membrane that regulates what enters and exits the self |
| Pitris | Progenitors | Progenitors / formative influences | The formative influences — internal or external — that shape a system's early architecture |
| Manu | Phase Regulator | The archetypal prototype | The prototype for a phase or cycle; the standard against which development is measured |
| Sishta | Preserved Seed | Remnant / saved seed | The preserved core knowledge or capacity that survives a collapse and seeds the next cycle |
| Deva | Operational Intelligence | Shining one / nature intelligence | A functional principle operating above the human scale; a pattern too large to be personalized |
